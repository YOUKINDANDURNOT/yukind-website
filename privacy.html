This policy explains what personal data YUKIND collects, why we need it, and how you stay in control of it.

Who we are

YUKIND is a London dog-care service. It runs the YUKIND app and yukind.uk. It is operated by [YUKIND LTD], company number [company number], registered at [registered address]. We are the controller of your personal data and are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office ([ICO registration number]).

Questions about your data: hello@yukind.uk.

What we collect

Everyone

  • Your name, email address and password (stored only in encrypted, hashed form).
  • Messages, photos and files you send in chat, and any reviews, reports or blocks you make.
  • Basic technical information, such as your device type, app version and error logs.

Dog owners

  • Details about your dog: name, breed, size, age, photo, care needs and notes.
  • Your bookings: dates, service, the sitters you contacted and the sitter you chose.
  • Payment records. Your card details are entered into and held by Stripe, our payment provider. We never see or store your full card number.

Sitters

  • Your profile: photo, bio, areas covered, prices, experience and the dog sizes you care for.
  • Your answers to our application questions.
  • Your DBS certificate, and the result of your identity check.
  • Payout details, which you give directly to Stripe. We receive only the status of your payout account.

Waitlist

  • Your first name, email address, borough, and whether you are an owner or a sitter.

DBS certificates

Information in a DBS certificate is criminal offence data. We use it only to decide whether a sitter can safely care for dogs in people's homes. Access is limited to the YUKIND staff who review applications, and we never show it to owners. Owners only see that a sitter's DBS check has been reviewed.

Who can see your data

  • Other YUKIND users. Owners see sitters' public profiles and reviews. Sitters see the dog details and messages in requests sent to them. Your email address is never shown to other users.
  • Our service providers, who process data on our behalf under contract: Supabase (app database, sign-in and file storage), Stripe (payments, payouts and identity checks), Apple (app distribution), Google (business email, and the fonts on this website).
  • Authorities, where the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

We never sell your personal data.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. When they do, they use safeguards approved under UK data protection law, such as the International Data Transfer Agreement or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, for as long as it is open. You can delete it at any time in the app.
  • Payment and booking records, for 6 years, to meet UK tax and accounting rules.
  • DBS certificates, while your sitter account is active, and deleted within 30 days of closing it.
  • Messages, reports and blocks, for up to 2 years after your last booking, so we can look into safety concerns.
  • The waitlist, until YUKIND opens near you, or until you ask us to remove you.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, or send it to another service. You can also object to how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time. Email hello@yukind.uk and we'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address. The app keeps you signed in by storing a secure sign-in token on your phone.

Children

YUKIND is for adults. You must be 18 or over to create an account.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app or by email before the change takes effect.
