What YUKIND does

YUKIND is a marketplace. It helps dog owners find, message and book independent dog sitters in London, and handles payment between them.

Sitters are self-employed. They are not employed by YUKIND, and they decide which requests to accept and what to charge. When an owner confirms a sitter, the care agreement is between the owner and the sitter. YUKIND provides the platform, the payment service and support.

Sitters

Bookings and payment

  • Sending requests and chatting are free. You're charged only when you confirm a sitter.

  • Payments are processed by Stripe. Sitters receive their full price after the care is complete, paid to their bank through Stripe.

Cancellations and refunds

  • Owners who cancel 48 hours or more before the booking starts get a full refund. For later cancellations, contact hello@yukind.uk and we'll look at each case fairly.
  • If a sitter cancels, the owner gets a full refund, including the booking fee.
  • If something goes wrong during a booking, tell us within 48 hours of the booking ending, and we will look into it.
  • Refunds go back to the original card, usually within 5 to 10 working days.
  • Your 14-day right to cancel. Under UK consumer law you can cancel a booking within 14 days of making it for a full refund. If you book care that starts within those 14 days, you're asking for the care to begin early, and the 48-hour rule above applies instead.
