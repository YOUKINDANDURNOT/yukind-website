Why sit with us

  1. Your price

    Set a price for each kind of care. It's yours in full.

  2. Your time

    Answer each request with "I'm free" or "not this time". No pressure, no penalties.

  3. Standby

    Turn on Standby to pick up urgent bookings when another sitter cancels. Already paid, ready to go.

Questions

Does it cost anything to join?

No. YUKIND is free for sitters. Owners pay a booking fee on top of your price.

When do I get paid?

When you mark the care as done, your pay is sent to your bank account through Stripe.

What if an owner cancels late?

Owners get a refund only if they cancel 48 hours or more before. After that, you're protected.

What if I have to cancel?

Life happens. Cancel in the app and we ask trusted backup sitters straight away, so the dog is looked after.

I have a dog too. Can I book sitters?

Yes. One account works for both. Switch between sitter and owner in your account.
