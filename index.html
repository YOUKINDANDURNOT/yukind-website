The care

Four kinds of care, each priced by your sitter. You see the full amount before you pay, and nothing is charged until you confirm.

Walk

Time outside with a sitter who knows your dog's pace. Sniffs encouraged, muddy paws towelled.

Per walk, morning, afternoon or evening

Day sitting

Company for the hours you're away, at your home. Walks, meals and naps kept to your routine.

Per day

Overnight

A sitter stays with your dog while you travel, so nights and mornings feel ordinary.

Per night

Special care

For older dogs, medication, recovery after surgery or nervous temperaments. Matched only with experienced sitters.

Per hour

How to book

  1. Describe your dog

    Size, age, habits, and anything a stranger should know: medication, nerves, a favourite toy.

  2. Shortlist up to five sitters

    We show only sitters who suit your dog and cover your area. Each receives a short note from you.

  3. Talk, then choose

    Sitters who are free will reply. Chat with them, then confirm the one you trust.

  4. Pay once you've chosen

    Your card is charged only at this point. The other sitters are thanked and let go, automatically.

On kindness

YUKIND is named after Yuki, a white dog with strong opinions about long grass and pebble beaches.

We started by caring for dogs we knew, and noticed what owners worried about most: who is this person, will they follow the routine, and will my dog come home happy. YUKIND is built around those three questions.

Lana, founder

Our standards

Before a sitter can accept a single booking, they pass each of these.

Identity

Every sitter confirms who they are with photo ID.

DBS check

Each sitter provides a DBS certificate, read by a person at YUKIND.

Judgement

Sitters answer real situations: a dog slips its lead, feels unwell, or meets another dog badly.

Honest reviews

Only owners who have booked a sitter can review them.

Questions

When will YUKIND open?

In November 2026, across London, on iPhone. Join the waitlist and we'll write to you on the day.

How much does it cost?

Each sitter sets their own price. A booking fee is added for owners, and you see the total before paying.

When am I charged?

Only when you confirm a sitter. Sending requests and chatting are free.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel 48 hours or more before the booking for a full refund. If a sitter cancels, you are always refunded in full.

Can a sitter care for a dog with medical needs?

Yes. Sitters tell us about their experience with medication, older dogs and recovery, and only suitable sitters are shown to you.
